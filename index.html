01关于我About

我是中国人民大学高瓴人工智能学院长聘副教授，研究方向为算法博弈论、机制设计与拍卖。

I am a tenured associate professor at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China. My research interests include algorithmic game theory, mechanism design, and auctions.
